```python
def merge_sorted_lists(list1, list2):
    """
    Merges two sorted lists into one sorted list.
    
    Args:
        list1: The first sorted list.
        list2: The second sorted list.
    
    Returns:
        The merged sorted list.
    """
    result = []
    i, j = 0, 0
    
    # Merge the two lists by comparing elements
    while i < len(list1) and j < len(list2):
        if list1[i] <= list2[j]:
            result.append(list1[i])
            i += 1
        else:
            result.append(list2[j])
            j += 1
    
    # Append the remaining elements from list1 (if any)
    while i < len(list1):
        result.append(list1[i])
        i += 1
    
    # Append the remaining elements from list2 (if any)
    while j < len(list2):
        result.append(list2[j])
        j += 1
    
    return result
```

### 1. **Egg Rice with Vegetables (Simple & Balanced)**
**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup cooked rice
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup chopped vegetables (e.g., bell peppers, broccoli, carrots, or spinach)
- 1 tsp soy sauce (optional, for flavor)
- 1 tsp sesame oil (optional, for aroma and flavor)
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Cook the rice and let it cool slightly.
2. In a pan, heat a little oil or sesame oil.
3. Add the chopped vegetables and sauté for 3–5 minutes until tender.
4. Crack the eggs into the pan and scramble them gently.
5. Add the cooked rice and stir to combine.
6. Season with soy sauce and salt, and serve hot.

- 1 cup cooked rice
- 2 large eggs

**Instructions:**
